# Devices and Sync

Northwind Notes runs on the web, iOS, Android, macOS, and Windows.

## How many devices

The Free plan syncs across 2 devices. The Pro plan syncs across unlimited devices. If you go over the limit on Free, the oldest device is signed out.

## Offline mode

Notes are stored locally and sync when you reconnect. Offline mode is available on mobile and desktop apps but not on the web app.

## Sync conflicts

If the same note is edited on two devices while offline, Northwind keeps both versions and marks the newer one as the main copy. The older version is saved in the note history for 90 days.

## Exporting your notes

You can export all notes as Markdown or PDF from Settings, then Export. Exports are emailed as a zip file and the download link expires after 7 days.
