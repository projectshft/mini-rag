# Refund Policy

Northwind Notes is a fictional note-taking app used as sample data for this course.

## Refund window

You can request a full refund within 30 days of your first payment. After 30 days, refunds are only issued if the service was unavailable for more than 24 hours in a single billing period.

## How to request a refund

Email support@northwind.example with your account email and the last four digits of the card you paid with. Refund requests are processed within 5 business days. The money goes back to the original payment method.

## Annual plans

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days. After that, we refund the unused months minus a 10 percent processing fee.

## What is not refundable

Gift cards, add-on storage packs, and team seats that were removed mid-cycle are not refundable.
