# Auth three ways: decision memo

Answer each in a short paragraph, in your own words. Say what each choice costs.

## 1. Sessions or tokens?

If this app had one web front end and nothing else, which would you use and why?
What would make you change your mind?

## 2. Logging people out

How does logout work in your session version, your token version and your
provider version? Which one can cut off a stolen login fastest?

## 3. Build or buy?

Compare your own login with the provider. Cover at least: what you no longer have
to get right yourself, what it costs per month at 1,000 and at 100,000 users,
what happens to your app when the provider is down, and how hard it would be to
leave.

## 4. What still belongs to you

Which parts of "who may do what" could the provider never do for you, and why?

## 5. The attack you nearly missed

Which hidden-test category, or which attack from the README, did your first
version get wrong? What was the fix?
